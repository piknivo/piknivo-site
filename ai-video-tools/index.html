AI Video Tools: Guides & Comparisons

Heads up: Piknivo may earn a commission if you buy through links on this page, at no extra cost to you. It doesn't change our recommendations. Learn more.

AI video tools turn a script, a prompt, or an existing video into something you can publish, often without a camera or a studio. They fall into a few groups, and the right one depends on what you make.

The main types of AI video tools

Type What it does Good for
AI avatar presenters Type a script; a realistic AI presenter reads it on screen Training, onboarding, explainers, sales videos
Prompt-to-video makers Describe a video; the tool assembles scenes, voiceover, and music Social clips, ads, YouTube content
AI dubbing and translation Re-voice a video in other languages, often with lip-sync Reaching global audiences
Transcript-based editors Edit video and audio by editing the text transcript Podcasts, interviews, talking-head videos

How to choose

  • Know your output. Training modules, social clips, and translated ads need different tools.
  • Check the free plan first. Most tools offer one, usually with a watermark and tight limits.
  • Read how credits work. Many AI video tools now bill in credits, and minutes per credit can vary a lot.
  • Confirm your languages before you pay for dubbing or translation.

Both Synthesia and HeyGen let you type a script and get back a video of a realistic AI presenter. No camera, no studio, no retakes. But they're built for different people. Synthesia leans toward corporate training and learning teams. HeyGen leans toward creators, marketers, and anyone translating video for a global audience.

Both companies also changed their plans in 2026. Synthesia renamed its Creator plan to Pro on October 1, 2026, and HeyGen moved to credit-based plans. A lot of older comparisons are now out of date. Here's where things actually stand.

Quick verdict

Choose Synthesia if… - You make training, onboarding, or compliance videos and need them to work inside an LMS (Synthesia documents integrations with Docebo, Articulate 360, SAP Litmos, Moodle, Workday, and more). - You want a free plan with real minutes: 10 minutes of video (or dubbing) every month. - You want the lowest annual entry price: Starter is $18/month billed yearly. - You want interactive training features like quizzes, clickable CTAs, and branching.

Choose HeyGen if… - You're a solo creator or marketer making social, sales, or YouTube videos. - You need 4K export without an enterprise contract. It starts on the $49/month Pro plan. - Translation and dubbing are central to what you do. Paid plans list 175+ languages and dialects. - You want an API you can use without a subscription: HeyGen's API is pay-as-you-go.

Cons - Free plan videos carry a watermark, and downloading appears to require a paid plan - Starter adds only about 12 minutes of extra video per month - No 4K mentioned. Who should pick which?

L&D and HR teams: Synthesia's documentation is built around training: LMS embeds, SCORM, quizzes, branching, and HRIS integrations. HeyGen also offers SCORM and interactive video, but only from its Business plan up. If your videos live in an LMS, start your trial with Synthesia.

Solo creators and social marketers: HeyGen's Creator plan gives you 30-minute videos, 1080p export, watermark removal, and voice cloning for $29/month ($24 billed annually). If you'll want 4K later, Pro costs $49.

Localization-heavy teams: Both dub with lip-sync. HeyGen lists more languages on its paid plans (175+). Synthesia dubs into 70+ languages on self-serve plans and 140+ on Enterprise. Before you commit, check that your target languages are supported.

Developers: HeyGen's pay-as-you-go API means you don't need a plan. Synthesia's Video API needs Pro or higher, but includes 360 minutes a year.

Just exploring on a $0 budget: Synthesia's free plan offers more minutes (10/month). HeyGen's free plan lets you create a custom avatar and try Avatar IV, but caps each video at 1 minute.

Alternatives worth a look

  • InVideo AI: an AI video creation and editing platform that uses AI agents and a range of generative models to build and edit videos from prompts (invideo.io).
  • Descript: an AI video and audio editor that lets you edit by editing the transcript, with a free plan available (descript.com).

FAQ

Is there a free version of Synthesia or HeyGen? Yes. Synthesia's Basic plan is free and includes 10 minutes of video or dubbing per month. HeyGen's Free plan includes up to 3 videos per month (1–3 depending on region), each up to 1 minute. Both add a watermark to free videos.

Which is cheaper, Synthesia or HeyGen? Monthly, both entry plans cost $29. Billed yearly, Synthesia Starter is $18/month vs. HeyGen Creator at $24/month. At the next tier up, HeyGen Pro starts at $49/month and Synthesia Pro is $89/month ($64 billed yearly). What you actually get for the money depends on how many minutes each plan's credits cover for your type of video.

Can I create an AI avatar of myself? Yes, on both. Synthesia offers Personal Avatars on Starter and above (3 on Starter, 5 on Pro). HeyGen includes 1 custom video avatar even on the Free plan, and more on paid plans (the slot count varies by account).

Does either tool export in 4K? HeyGen does, on its Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans. Synthesia's pricing page lists Full HD 1080p downloads. We found no mention of 4K.

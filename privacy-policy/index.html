Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Piknivo ("Piknivo," "we," "us") collects and uses information when you visit piknivo.com (the "Site"). Piknivo is based in São José dos Campos, São Paulo, Brazil.

Information we collect

You give us directly: If you email us or use a contact or newsletter form, we receive your name, email address, and whatever you include in your message.

Collected automatically: Like most websites, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the Site. It may collect your approximate location (city/region level), device and browser type, pages viewed, time on page, the site that referred you, and clicks on links (including affiliate links). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Affiliate partners: When you click an affiliate link, the partner's website may place its own cookie to record that you came from Piknivo. That partner's privacy policy applies once you're on its site.

How we use information

  • To run, maintain, and improve the Site
  • To understand which content is useful
  • To reply to your messages
  • To send our newsletter, only if you sign up (you can unsubscribe anytime)

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Learn how Google uses data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California and other U.S. states with privacy laws, or the EU/UK), you may have the right to know, access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of certain uses such as targeted advertising. To make a request, contact us at piknivo@gmail.com. We'll respond within the time the law requires and won't treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Data retention and security

We keep Google Analytics data for up to 14 months. We keep emails only as long as needed to respond or as you request. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website is 100% secure.

Third-party links

The Site links to other websites. We aren't responsible for their privacy practices.

International visitors

Piknivo is operated from Brazil and uses service providers (such as Google and GitHub) that may process data in the United States and other countries.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.

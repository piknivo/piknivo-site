How We Review

Piknivo exists to help you choose software with confidence. Here's exactly how our pages are made, so you can judge them for yourself.

Where our facts come from

  • Official sources first: pricing pages, help centers, documentation, and changelogs from the company that makes the tool.
  • Every page shows a date for when we last checked prices and plans. Software changes often, so always confirm on the official site before you buy.
  • We link our sources so you can check them yourself.

Two kinds of pages

  • Research-based: built from official sources only. We label these clearly and don't include scores, speed tests, or quality ratings.
  • Hands-on tested: we've used the tool ourselves. These pages say what we tested, on which plan, and when.

Right now, every page on Piknivo is research-based. We'll mark a page as hands-on tested only after we've actually done the testing.

What we never do

  • Invent test results, ratings, reviews, or testimonials
  • Accept payment for a recommendation or a better ranking
  • Hide affiliate links. Every page that has them says so near the top

Corrections

Spotted an outdated price or a mistake? Email piknivo@gmail.com with the page and what changed. We'll check it against the official source and update the page.
